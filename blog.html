TECHNICAL SEO

What Is a Technical SEO Audit?

A technical SEO audit is a review of the technical parts of a website that can affect crawling, indexing, performance, and search visibility.

Common checks include broken links, redirects, site structure, mobile usability, page speed, robots.txt, XML sitemaps, and indexability.



KEYWORD RESEARCH

How to Find SEO Keywords

Keyword research is the process of finding words and phrases that people use when searching for information, products, or services.

Good keyword research considers search intent, competition, relevance, search volume, and the purpose of the website.



ON-PAGE SEO

How to Optimize a Web Page

On-page SEO involves improving the content and HTML elements of individual pages.

Important elements include the page title, meta description, headings, content, URLs, images, internal links, and structured data where appropriate.



LOCAL SEO

What Is Local SEO?

Local SEO focuses on helping businesses appear when people search for products or services connected to a specific location.

Location pages, business information, reviews, local content, citations, and a Google Business Profile can all be important parts of a local SEO strategy.



GEO

What Is Generative Engine Optimization?

Generative Engine Optimization, commonly called GEO, is the practice of improving how information is presented so generative search systems can understand the subject and context of a website.

Clear answers, useful original information, strong topical coverage, structured content, and trustworthy sources can help make information easier for search systems to interpret.



AEO

What Is Answer Engine Optimization?

Answer Engine Optimization, or AEO, focuses on providing direct answers to questions people ask.

Well-organized headings, question-based sections, concise answers, helpful explanations, and structured information can make content easier to understand.
