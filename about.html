WHO WE ARE

We start by understanding the website.

SEO is more than adding keywords to a page. A website needs to be accessible to search engines, useful to visitors, and organized in a way that makes its content easy to understand.

Our process starts with reviewing the technical setup, existing content, search terms, competitors, and the goals of the business.
